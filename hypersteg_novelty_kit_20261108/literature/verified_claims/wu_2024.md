## Claim 6: Generation — an embedding prompt writes the sentence; extraction by the LLM is imperfect, so a feedback loop of rejection sampling rewrites prompts until the keywords come back

> "we use an embedding prompt to induce LLMs to generate stego texts."

> "Unlike an explicit extraction algorithm, the extraction relied on the LLM cannot ensure a perfectly accurate extraction."

> "If the output keywords have errors, the LLM could return the main reason for these errors and optimize the embedding, generation, and extraction prompts until there are no extraction errors."

> "In our experiments, making two rejecting samples ensures the accurate extraction of secret messages embedded into each stego sentence."
