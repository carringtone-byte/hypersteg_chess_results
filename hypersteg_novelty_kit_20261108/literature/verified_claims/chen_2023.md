## Claim 5: Locating the mark without a synchronisation code — pattern bits plus a sliding brute-force decode, made tolerant by a shift module

> "The essence involves constructing the encoded message by combining pattern bits and payloads."

> "The pattern bits are used as the criterion for checking the validity of the decoded outputs."

> "As a result, our model becomes capable of decoding the watermark as long as the decoding position falls within 10% Encoding Unit Length (EUL) distance from the actual watermark position."

> "And when the shift length reaches 35% EUL, the performance is close to random guessing."
