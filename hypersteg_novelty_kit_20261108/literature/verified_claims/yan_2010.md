Source: Jeff Yan, Newcastle University, in *Proceedings of the Twenty-Fourth AAAI Conference on

## Claim 5: Detector — an inference engine flags actions too good to be drawn from partial information: in the collusive candidate set, not the honest one

> "Actions that are based on a decision deemed too good to be drawn from partial information will be detected as suspicious play."

> "if information that one can collect from his or her own hand and from others’ bids cannot justify the contract bid or opening lead, the suspicion of collusion will be raised."
