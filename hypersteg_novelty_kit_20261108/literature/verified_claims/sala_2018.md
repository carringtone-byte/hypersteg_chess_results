## Claim 1: Approach — a combinatorial construction that embeds a tree in hyperbolic space with arbitrarily low distortion, without optimisation

> "Given a tree, we give a combinatorial construction that embeds the tree in hyperbolic space with arbitrarily low distortion without using optimization."

> "The approach proceeds in two phases: (1) we produce an embedding of a graph into a weighted tree, and (2) we embed that tree into the hyperbolic disk. In particular, we consider an extension of an elegant embedding of trees into the Poincaré disk by Sarkar  [ 32 ]"

> "It is both fast (nearly linear time) and has formal quality guarantees."

## Claim 5: Tradeoff law — precision grows with path length but only logarithmically with degree; about d bits for a distance d
