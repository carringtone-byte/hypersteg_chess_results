## Claim 4: Why not the tangent space — consecutive exponential and logarithmic maps cancel, reducing HNN-style adaptation to plain LoRA

> "This approach fails to fully capture the hyperbolic geometry, as the exponential and logarithmic maps are mutually inverse and can be canceled with consecutive operations"

> "The cancellation effect occurs because standard hyperbolic neural network  Ganea et al. (2018b) ;  Chami et al. (2019)  approaches apply transformations in the tangent space at the origin"

> "This process results in consecutive logarithmic and exponential mappings"

> "which effectively cancel each other out, reducing the method to the original LoRA approach and nullifying any benefits from hyperbolic geometry."

**ID:** tangent-space-cancellation
**Location:** Section 1, "Challenges" and footnote 3; Section 5 (the "standard hyperbolic neural network" citation is read here as Ganea, Bécigneul and Hofmann, *Hyperbolic neural networks*, NeurIPS; the extract's reference list does not mark which Ganea 2018 entry is "2018b")
