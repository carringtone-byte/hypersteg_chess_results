Codetta is a cryptographic (provably distribution-preserving) token-sampling channel between
two LLM agents whose models and private contexts differ, with a covert key exchange so that
no pre-shared key is needed. For the Hypersteg map it belongs to the provably secure
sampling-based family (Hopper–Langford–von Ahn, Meteor, Discop) extended to the asymmetric,
keyless case, not to prompted natural-language schemes.

## Claim 1: Problem — existing provably secure LLM steganography assumes symmetry and a pre-shared key, and the asymmetric protocol has very low capacity

> "High-capacity schemes typically assume a symmetric setting where the receiver can reproduce the sender’s output distribution."

> "The state-of-the-art practical protocol for asymmetric agents has very low capacity."

> "In our experiments, however, its capacity is below  0.025 0.025  bits per token. Transmitting a  1024 1024 -bit message therefore requires hundreds of thousands of tokens, making such collusion impractical."

> "So the paradox is that the agents need a covert channel to establish the key and a key to construct the covert channel."

## Claim 5: Encoding (keyed) — a public draft model partitions the vocabulary into buckets, and maximal coupling samples the token exactly from the sender's distribution

## Claim 13: Approach — Codetta's keyed protocol, high-capacity asymmetric steganography through a public draft model and maximal coupling
