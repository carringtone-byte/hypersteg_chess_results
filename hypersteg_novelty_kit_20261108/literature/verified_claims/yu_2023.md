## Claim 3: Keys — two conditions on a shared pre-trained model; the private key describes the secret, the public key the container

> "For the private key, it determines whether the receiver can successfully reveal the original image, so it cannot be transmitted over public channels."

## Claim 5: Security argument — wrong private keys still yield plausible images, and the container carries no explicit clues
