## Claim 10: Approach — RL fine-tuning with an outcome reward minus a rule-based CoT penalty on task-critical strings

> "In each case, we implement RL fine-tuning using a reward composed of two elements: (i)  r task r_{\text{task}} , a binary, outcome-based task reward for correctly solving the reasoning task, and (ii)  p CoT p_{\text{CoT}} , a CoT penalty obtained from a rule-based monitor, which penalizes the use of strings that would normally carry information critical for solving the task."

> "This approach is intended to simulate the impact of applying optimization pressure to the CoT during post-training of frontier LLMs."

> "Using this technique, we study responses from LLMs in three different settings, which we term  ‘Coin Flip’ ,  ‘Arithmetic’ , and  ‘Theory of Mind’ ."
