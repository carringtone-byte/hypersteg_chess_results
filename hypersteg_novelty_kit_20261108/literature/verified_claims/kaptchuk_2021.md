## Claim 13: Approach — Meteor: a symmetric-key, stateful, provably secure scheme that embeds message bits in the sampling randomness of a generative model, at a rate that follows the instantaneous entropy

> "Meteor. We present Meteor, a new symmetric-key, stateful, provably secure, steganographic system that naturally adapts to highly variable entropy."

> "Our key observation is that the best way to adapt to variable entropy is to fluidly change the encoding rate to be proportional to the instantaneous entropy."

> "Meteor embeds messages into the random number"
